DSG ONE product landing contract

- Root/index with no checkout state and no hash redirects to product.html.
- index.html#verify keeps the existing live verification page.
- ?checkout=success or ?checkout=cancelled keeps the existing commerce return flow.
- landing/index.html and azure-landing/index.html remain byte-identical.
- landing/product.html and azure-landing/product.html are intended to remain byte-identical.
