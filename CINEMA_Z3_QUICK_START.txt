  curl http://<Z3_SERVICE_URL>/health

Example:
  curl http://z3-solver-service.westus3.azurecontainer.io:8080/health

Expected response:
  {"status": "ok", "version": "4.13.0", ...}

❌ If you get error:
   - Connection refused → Z3 not running
   - 404 Not Found → Wrong URL
   - 401 Unauthorized → Wrong API_SECRET

⚠️  DO NOT PROCEED until Z3 responds with {"status": "ok"}
