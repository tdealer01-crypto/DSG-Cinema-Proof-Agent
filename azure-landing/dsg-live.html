Evidence, not screenshots of logs

Every status has a proof boundary.

DSG keeps claimed, observed, and proved states separate. Replay is verification only: it compares recorded outputs and evidence and never re-sends the action or creates another production side effect.

CLAIMEDCaller or agent supplied it. Not independently demonstrated yet.
OBSERVEDBacked by stored execution records or evidence artifacts.
PROVEDRecomputed by DSG and bound to a verified proof receipt.
REPLAY VERIFIEDVerification-only comparison. No execution replay or repeated side effect.
