Proof Receipt

A result customers can inspect, store, replay and meter.

DSG returns proof only after the exact verifier confirms the bounded policy decision. This example shows the receipt shape, not a live production receipt.

Plan-boundApproved plan and proposed action are represented by canonical hashes.
Exact verificationReceipt requires verified=true and VERIFIED_GLOBAL_OPTIMUM from the Z3 path.
ReplayableReplay match, request hash, proof hash and context hash remain inspectable.
Business-readyEvidence completeness and cost/run sit beside the verification result.
